Columbus, Ohio Tax Deduction Engine

Detailed breakdown of all municipal, state, federal, and payroll tax deductions applied to your $12.00/hr wage in Columbus, Franklin County, Ohio.

Columbus City Income Tax

2.50% Flat

The City of Columbus levies a mandatory municipal income tax rate of 2.50% on all gross compensation earned by individuals working within the city limits of Columbus, OH. Unlike state or federal taxes, there is no standard deduction for city income tax.

At $12.00/hr: $12.00 × 2.50% = $0.30/hr deducted

Federal FICA (SS & Medicare)

7.65% Total

Federal Insurance Contributions Act (FICA) consists of:
• Social Security (OASDI): 6.20%
• Medicare Hospital Insurance: 1.45%
Totaling 7.65% on all earned wages. (Can be exempted in Settings if on F-1 non-resident student visa).

At $12.00/hr: $12.00 × 7.65% = $0.92/hr deducted

Ohio State Income Tax (SIT)

~1.10% Estimated

Ohio graduated income tax has zero tax on the first $26,050 of taxable income and 2.75% over that amount. For an hourly wage of $12/hr, typical withholding or effective liability averages around 1.10%.

At $12.00/hr: $12.00 × 1.10% = $0.13/hr deducted

Federal Income Tax (FIT)

~4.25% Estimated

Accounting for the single filer standard deduction (~$14,600/year), estimated effective federal withholding for full/part-time $12.00/hr earnings averages approximately 4.25%.

At $12.00/hr: $12.00 × 4.25% = $0.51/hr deducted
